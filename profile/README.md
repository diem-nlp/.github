# DIEM-THESES

This organization collects educational and scientific material in the fields of **Machine Learning**, **Natural Language Processing**, and **Software Engineering**, developed within thesis projects, courses, and research activities of the *Department of Information Engineering, Electrical Engineering and Applied Mathematics (DIEM)* at the *University of Salerno*.

## What you will find here

- Bachelor's and Master's thesis projects
- Source code of research prototypes and experimental studies
- Datasets, benchmarks, and evaluation tools
- Research papers, technical reports, and supplementary material
- NLP pipelines and reference architectures for academic use

## Purpose

The organization aims to foster:

- collaboration among students and with faculty members;
- transparency and reproducibility of experimental results;
- sharing and reuse of educational and scientific resources;
- continuity between course projects, thesis work, and research activities.

## Access policy

Most repositories are **private** and restricted to members of the organization. Public repositories are limited to material supporting scientific publications (e.g., replication packages).
